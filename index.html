Model & coordinate conventions

Positions are in mm; bench dimensions are in cm. Mirror, grating and lens orientation is the surface normal: azimuth is measured from +X toward +Y; elevation tilts it toward +Z; roll rotates aperture axes and grating grooves about the normal. Laser angles describe its propagation direction. Fine tilt uses local U/V axes.

The simulation uses geometric rays, finite apertures, vector reflection and a selected reflective grating order. The lens supports exact spherical/planar surface refraction or a paraxial thin lens. The Gaussian source samples position and angular spread; beam radius and divergence are 1/e² values. Bandwidth is spectral FWHM. Coating efficiencies are scalar inputs. Boundary power sums surviving sampled power at any wall of the work volume.

Physical Optics adds scalar diffraction on a sampled grid; Pulse & Dispersion adds traced spectral phase and a temporal FFT; Polarization tracks Jones vectors. These analyses have their own sampling and model limits. Mechanical mount volumes and nonlinear THz generation are not modeled. Glass indices use the existing N-LAK22/N-SF6 dispersion data, or your supplied constant indices. The example layout is illustrative. Intersections within lens glass are traced as one element; keep separate components clear of the glass volume.

Chief-ray encounter details

Beam on the optical surface

Weighted geometric hit density in local U/V coordinates. The outline is the clear aperture; red crosses are clipped; scatter points use the wavelength colours. Lens interfaces and repeated visits are recorded separately. Only optical-element surfaces are shown.

Grating incidence, diffraction & deviation

Deviation compares the outgoing beam with the return direction of the incoming beam, projected perpendicular to the grooves. The solver holds the groove direction, wavelength, order, density and all other components fixed. Run a trace that reaches the grating first.

Focus after the selected element

Find the smallest power-weighted RMS spot for each wavelength and the combined beam. The search ends before the next optic or bench boundary. Increase source rays and wavelength samples to check stability. These are geometric spots; diffraction and pulse compression require the other analysis tabs.

Geometric spot at the combined minimum

Centred on the combined centroid. Spot spread includes lens aberrations, source divergence and alignment. Up to 1,600 representative rays are displayed; all surviving rays enter the calculations.

Ray & wavelength convergence

Checks incident power, centroid and beam radii at the selected surface. Two successful refinements are required. Ray count and spectral sampling increase together.

Physical model checks

Numerical convergence measures sampling stability. Model verification also uses reflection, grating momentum, Snell reciprocity, optical invariants, Gaussian propagation, polarization power, grating spectral phase and pulse broadening benchmarks. Calibration to your hardware requires measured source and component data.

Alignment sensitivity at this surface

Central differences for X/Y/Z, local U/V tilt and roll. The selected target coordinates stay fixed. A missed target or changed route invalidates that derivative.

Coherent field on this element

A two-component Jones field follows the chief route using padded angular-spectrum propagation. Apertures diffract and gratings remap the pupil. Doublets use wavelength-dependent first-order ABCD equivalents. Exact geometric rays remain the separate model for higher-order lens aberrations. Envelope phase excludes the rapidly varying chief-ray carrier.

Pulse at the selected surface

Calculation settings · surface point, dispersion & fibre

Instantaneous wavelength shows chirp where pulse intensity exceeds 0.1% of its peak. It is undefined in dark portions of the pulse.

The phase includes traced geometric/material optical path, grating phase ramps, coating phase, source GDD/TOD and optional supplied fibre dispersion. Source spectrum sets the transform-limited reference. Enable local field sampling to include spatial spectral filtering at the chosen U/V point; otherwise the pulse describes the tracked chief-ray family. This model does not predict nonlinear THz emission.

Polarization along the beam

Jones vectors follow each 3D ray; Stokes values describe the transverse beam basis. Mirrors and gratings use supplied S/P attenuation and phase, not a calculated microscopic coating response. Polarizers and waveplates are available in the product library. Bare Fresnel transmission is optional for surface-traced lenses.
